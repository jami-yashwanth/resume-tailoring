Backend Engineer — Kosha Payments
Pune (on-site, 5 days a week)

About the role
Kosha Payments processes UPI and card settlements for merchants across India.
We are looking for a backend engineer to join the payments platform team.

What you will do
- Build and maintain payment and settlement services in Java and Spring Boot.
- Own REST APIs used by merchant-facing products and internal tooling.
- Work on our event-driven ledger, which runs on Kafka.
- Run services on AWS, deployed as microservices on ECS and EKS.
- Mentor junior engineers on the team and review their code.

What we are looking for
- 3+ years building backend services in Java.
- Strong Spring Boot background.
- Solid AWS experience.
- Experience designing and consuming REST APIs.
- Experience with microservices architectures.
- Apache Kafka in production. This is a must have for this role.
- Kubernetes is a nice to have.
- Experience mentoring or leading junior engineers.
- Based in Pune, or willing to relocate. This role is on-site.

Benefits
Health insurance for you and your family, an annual learning budget, and
catered lunch. Kosha Payments is an equal opportunity employer.
