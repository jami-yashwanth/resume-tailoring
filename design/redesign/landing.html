Questions

Will it add skills I don't have?

Only if you say so. When a job asks for a skill that isn't in your resume, we draft the line, mark it “Not in your resume” and ask. It goes in only if you choose Add it, and we give you interview prep for it afterwards.

Do you give an ATS score?

No. Applicant tracking systems don't publish a score, so any number would be invented. We show whether your file parses cleanly and which of the job's requirements it covers.

Will my resume look different?

No. We edit your own file and keep its fonts and layout. You download it as PDF or DOCX.

Does the pass renew?

No. You pay once by UPI. When the 30 or 90 days are over, it stops. There is nothing to cancel.

Do I need to sign up?

Not to try. You sign in with your phone number and a one-time code only when you download.

What happens to my resume data?

Your resume is used only to tailor your resumes. It is stored encrypted, never sold, and never used to train AI models. You can delete everything from Settings at any time. [Draft: confirm storage region and deletion time.]
